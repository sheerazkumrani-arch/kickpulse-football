Europa League scores and why they matter

The UEFA Europa League is Europe's second club competition. Clubs that finish just below the Champions League places in their home leagues play here, together with teams that drop down from the Champions League. Like the Champions League, it now uses a 36-team league phase, followed by play-offs and knockout rounds. The winner earns a place in the next Champions League, which gives every match extra weight.

Games are normally played on Thursday nights, with two kick-off slots so fans can follow more than one match. Use the Live tab to see what is being played right now, and the date bar to look at the next Thursday fixtures. If a club you support has a European night coming up, search for its name and the game will appear on the list.

Tap a match to read the timeline of goals and cards and to compare team statistics. The competition produces plenty of surprises and a few lively away trips, and a quick glance at the live football score list is often the best way to catch them.

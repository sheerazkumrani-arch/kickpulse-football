About UEFA Europa League live scores

The UEFA Europa League is UEFA's second club competition, played by strong clubs from across Europe. Matches are usually played on Thursday evenings in Europe. Open any match on this page to see the timeline of goals and cards, team statistics and the lineups when they are available.

Teams from many countries meet in this competition, so the match list can include clubs you do not follow every week. Use the search box to find your club by name, or open the Stats tab during a live match to compare shots, possession and corners between the two sides.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Europa League scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Europa League match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

When are Europa League matches played?

Matches are normally played in European evenings, usually on Thursdays. Pick a date at the top of the page to see the fixtures and results for that day.

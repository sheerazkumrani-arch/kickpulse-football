How to read MLS results

The first team listed is the home club and the second is the away club. In the regular season a level score after full time is a draw. In the playoffs, games that must produce a winner can go to extra time and penalties, which are covered on MLS playoffs matches today.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I see MLS results from yesterday?

The list on this page groups the latest MLS results by match day, with the newest day first. Yesterday's final scores are in the first groups under the summary at the top.

What were the MLS results last night?

Last night's final scores are at the top of the results list. The summary sentence names the most recent match day and its first results, and the list below shows every finished game.

Can an MLS game end in a draw?

Yes. In the regular season a game that is level after 90 minutes plus stoppage time ends as a draw. Playoff games that need a winner can go to extra time and penalties.

How often are MLS results updated?

Results come from ESPN's public data feed and appear here shortly after a match ends. They can be a little delayed or occasionally unavailable.

Where can I see the MLS standings?

The Eastern and Western Conference tables are on the MLS standings page. They update after every game.

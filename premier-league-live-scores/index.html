About English Premier League live scores

The English Premier League is the top division of football in England, with 20 clubs playing from August to May. Most matches are played at weekends, with extra rounds in midweek. Tap any match on this page for the timeline of goals and cards, team statistics such as possession and shots, and the lineups when they are available.

A typical match week has games on Saturday and Sunday, with some on Friday or Monday. Kick-off times are shown in your device's time zone, so an evening match in England appears at a later local hour in Pakistan. The Upcoming tab lists matches that have not started, and the Finished tab lists completed results. Arsenal, Chelsea, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United and Tottenham are among the best-known clubs in the competition.

Following the Premier League day by day

If you only have a minute, the Live tab on this page is the quickest way to check the football match today and see which Premier League games are in progress. It lists every game that is being played, with the minute and the score, and it refreshes by itself. When no game is live, the Upcoming tab shows what is next, and the Finished tab gives the full-time scores from earlier in the day.

The league is a 38-game marathon for each club, which means a typical season produces 380 matches. That is why there is almost always something to follow: a Saturday afternoon slate, a Sunday big game, a Monday night fixture and midweek rounds squeezed in around cup football. Whether you support one of the so-called big clubs or a team fighting to stay up, the live score page works the same way. Search for the club and the match jumps to the top of the list.

The top of the table is the story most people watch, but the bottom three clubs go down to the Championship at the end of the season, and those relegation battles often produce the most dramatic Saturday afternoons. Keeping an eye on every live score at once is the easiest way to feel the tension.

Is the EPL table updated live?

Yes. The table below the matches reloads about once a minute, so points and positions change after goals and final whistles without you refreshing the page. Because the numbers come from a public feed, they can occasionally lag a little behind the stadium.

What time do Premier League matches start in Pakistan, India or Nigeria?

A 3 pm kick-off in England is 8 pm in Pakistan, 8:30 pm in India and 4 pm in Nigeria during UK winter time. You do not need to calculate it: every time on the match list is already converted to your own time zone.

Premier League: quick facts and popular searches

The Premier League has 20 clubs, and a win is worth three points and a draw one point. When two clubs finish level on points, goal difference is checked next. The bottom three clubs are relegated to the Championship at the end of the season.

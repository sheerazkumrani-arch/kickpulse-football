About English Premier League live scores

The English Premier League is the top division of football in England, with 20 clubs playing from August to May. Most matches are played at weekends, with extra rounds in midweek. Tap any match on this page for the timeline of goals and cards, team statistics such as possession and shots, and the lineups when they are available.

A typical match week has games on Saturday and Sunday, with some on Friday or Monday. Kick-off times are shown in your device's time zone, so an evening match in England appears at a later local hour in Pakistan. The Upcoming tab lists matches that have not started, and the Finished tab lists completed results. Arsenal, Chelsea, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United and Tottenham are among the best-known clubs in the competition.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Premier League scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Premier League match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

Can I follow my favourite Premier League club here?

Yes. Type the club name in the search box at the top. Any club with a match on the selected date appears in the list, and you can use the date bar to find its next game.

Is KickPulse free to use for Premier League scores?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed and may be delayed or unavailable at times.

Ligue 1 live scores from France

Ligue 1 is the top division of French football. It has 18 clubs and a 34-match season, and it has produced many of the young players who later light up bigger stages. Paris Saint-Germain are the club most people think of first, but Marseille, Lyon, Monaco, Lille and Lens bring their own history and loud supporters. The match between PSG and Marseille is known as Le Classique and is the biggest night of the French calendar.

French football often plays in the evening, including Friday night games, so the local kick-off time can matter a lot to you depending on where you are. This page converts every time to your time zone. You can see matches that are live now, results from earlier, and fixtures ahead, and you can search for any team by name.

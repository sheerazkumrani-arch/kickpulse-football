About French Ligue 1 live scores

Ligue 1 is the top division of French football, with 18 clubs. The season runs from August to May. Follow the live score and match minute for every game on the selected date, and open a match to read its timeline of goals and cards and compare the team statistics.

Matches are spread across the weekend, so the match list is easiest to read when you use the status tabs. Choose Live to see games in progress, Upcoming for games that have not started, and Finished for completed results with the final score. Paris Saint-Germain, Marseille, Lyon, Monaco, Lille and Nice are among the clubs you can follow.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Ligue 1 scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Ligue 1 match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

What does HT or FT mean?

HT means half time and FT means full time. Live matches show the current minute instead.

Is KickPulse free to use for Ligue 1 scores?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed and may be delayed or unavailable at times.

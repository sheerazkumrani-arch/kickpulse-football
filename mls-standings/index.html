This page shows the MLS standings: the Eastern Conference table and the Western Conference table, with points, wins, draws, losses, goals scored and conceded, and goal difference for every club. The tables refresh after every match, and the summary sentence names the leaders.

How the MLS table is ordered

Clubs are ranked by points inside their own conference. A win earns 3 points, a draw 1 and a defeat none. Clubs near the top of each conference qualify for the MLS Cup Playoffs, and the club with the most points in the whole league wins the Supporters' Shield.

Frequently asked questions

How does the MLS table work?

MLS ranks clubs inside their conference, the Eastern Conference and the Western Conference, rather than in one single table. A win is worth 3 points, a draw 1 point and a loss 0. Clubs near the top of each conference qualify for the MLS Cup Playoffs.

Who is top of the MLS standings?

The summary at the top of this page names the leader of each conference and the club with the most points in MLS overall. It updates automatically as results come in.

Is there one MLS table or two?

There are two conference tables, Eastern and Western. The club with the most points across the whole league wins the Supporters' Shield.

What do P, W, D, L, GF, GA, GD and Pts mean?

P is games played, W wins, D draws, L losses, GF goals for, GA goals against, GD goal difference and Pts points.

How often is the MLS table updated?

The table comes from ESPN's public data feed and refreshes after every match. It can be a little delayed or occasionally unavailable.

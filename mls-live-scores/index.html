MLS and soccer in North America

Major League Soccer is the top professional soccer league in the United States and Canada. Clubs are divided into the Eastern and Western Conferences, and the season runs from February to the autumn, followed by the MLS Cup playoffs. The league has grown quickly, and the arrival of Lionel Messi at Inter Miami in 2023 brought a new wave of fans. LA Galaxy, Seattle Sounders, LAFC and Atlanta United are some of the clubs with big followings.

Because the matches are played across several North American time zones, kick-off times can look odd from elsewhere in the world. That is where the automatic time zone conversion on this page helps. In the United States and Canada the sport is called soccer, and the same games are called football in most other countries; if you searched for live soccer scores or soccer today, you are on the right page.

Tap any match to see the timeline of goals and cards, team statistics and lineups. For a wider look at the game under its American name, read our soccer live scores page.

Frequently asked questions

How often do MLS scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each MLS match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

Why do MLS matches show late-night times?

North America is many hours behind Pakistan, so evening kick-offs there appear at night or in the early morning in your local time.

Is KickPulse free to use for MLS scores?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed and may be delayed or unavailable at times.

When does the MLS season start and end?

The MLS regular season usually starts in February or March and ends in October, followed by the playoffs and the MLS Cup final.

Does Lionel Messi play in MLS?

Lionel Messi joined Inter Miami in 2023. To see whether his club has a match today, search for Inter Miami in the box at the top of the page.

MLS on TV: how to watch live

MLS live scores · MLS schedule · MLS on TV · MLS playoffs · Inter Miami · Blog: Pitchside Notes

Looking for MLS on TV? Where you can watch Major League Soccer depends on the country you are in, because broadcast rights are sold by region.

How to find where to watch MLS

  • Open the official Major League Soccer website and look for its how-to-watch or broadcast page for your country.
  • Check which TV channels and streaming services carry MLS where you live, as these change from season to season.
  • Check the kick-off time in your own time zone on our MLS schedule, since many games start late at night in Europe and Asia.

Follow the match while you watch

KickPulse does not show video. It shows live MLS scores, goals, cards, statistics and lineups, which is handy as a second screen.

Free or paid?

This depends on your country and the current rights. Some matches are on free television in some regions, while others need a paid subscription. Always use official sources and avoid unofficial streams.

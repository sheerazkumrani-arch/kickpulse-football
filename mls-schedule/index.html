How the MLS schedule works

Major League Soccer has about 30 clubs from the United States and Canada, split into the Eastern and Western Conferences. The regular season runs from February to the autumn, and each club usually plays 34 matches. After that the MLS Cup playoffs decide the champion; see MLS playoffs matches today.

When is El Clasico played?

El Clasico is the match between Real Madrid and Barcelona. They meet twice each La Liga season, once at each ground, and the dates are set by the league calendar.

La Liga: quick facts and popular searches

La Liga has 20 clubs and 38 rounds in a season. A win is worth three points and a draw one point, and clubs that finish level are separated by their head-to-head results first, which is different from the Premier League.

La Liga scores, and why the league is so easy to love

La Liga is the top division of Spanish football, with 20 clubs and a season that runs from August to May. The names that travel furthest are Real Madrid, Barcelona and Atletico Madrid, and the matches between them are among the most watched in the sport. The Madrid-Barcelona game, known as El Clasico, is one of those fixtures people plan their whole evening around, so it is no surprise that live score searches spike on those days.

Spanish football often kicks off later in the evening than other leagues, and some Sunday games start close to midnight for viewers in South Asia. Because this page shows kick-off times in your own time zone, you can see at a glance whether a match is worth staying up for. During the game, open the match panel for the timeline, the possession and shots numbers and the lineups.

For fans who miss the days when Lionel Messi played here, or who follow Real Madrid because of the long line of famous names who wore the white shirt, La Liga still has the clubs and the atmosphere that make a live score worth checking. We also have a separate page for Real Madrid live scores.

When is El Clasico played?

El Clasico is the match between Real Madrid and Barcelona. They meet twice each La Liga season, once at each ground, and the dates are set by the league calendar.

How many teams play in La Liga?

La Liga has 20 clubs. Each club plays 38 matches, and the bottom three go down to the second division at the end of the season.

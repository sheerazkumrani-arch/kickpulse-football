About Saudi Pro League live scores

The Saudi Pro League is the top division of football in Saudi Arabia, with 18 clubs. Saudi Arabia is only two hours behind Pakistan time, so many matches are played at convenient hours for viewers in Pakistan. Kick-off times on this page are shown in your own time zone.

Because the time difference is small, you can often follow a full match live without staying up late. Open a match to see the timeline of goals and cards, and use the Info tab for the stadium and kick-off details when they are available. Al Hilal, Al Nassr, Al Ittihad and Al Ahli are among the best-known clubs.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Saudi Pro League scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Saudi Pro League match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

Are the times shown in Pakistan time?

They are shown in the time zone of your device, so if your phone is set to Pakistan time you will see Pakistan time.

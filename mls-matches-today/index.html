How "today" works for MLS

Major League Soccer is played across the United States and Canada, so one match day can start in the afternoon on the east coast and finish late at night on the west coast. We count a match day by the US Eastern date. Scores come from ESPN's public data feed and refresh while the page is open; for minute-by-minute updates open MLS live scores.

MLS kick-off times in Pakistan

Pakistan does not use daylight saving time, so the gap to the United States changes twice a year: 10 hours ahead of US Eastern Standard Time and 9 hours ahead of Eastern Daylight Time. An evening game in the US therefore falls in the early morning in Pakistan, and games on the west coast can fall after sunrise. Frequently asked questions

How many MLS matches are there today?

The summary at the top of this page counts today's MLS matches and lists them with their kick-off times. If there is no game today, it shows the next MLS match day instead. Most MLS games are played on Saturdays and Sundays, with some midweek games.

Is there an MLS game tonight?

Check the table at the top of this page. It lists tonight's MLS games with the kick-off time in US Eastern time, US Pacific time, Pakistan time and your own time zone, and it shows the live score once a game starts.

What time do MLS games start?

Most MLS games kick off in the North American evening. Because the league covers several time zones, the same match day can have games starting at different clock times. Every time on this page is converted for you.

What time are MLS matches in Pakistan?

Pakistan is 10 hours ahead of US Eastern Standard Time and 9 hours ahead during US daylight saving time, so a US evening kick-off is usually in the early morning in Pakistan. A 7:30 PM Eastern kick-off is 5:30 AM in Pakistan in winter and 4:30 AM in summer.

Where can I watch MLS games today?

See our guide on how to watch MLS on TV for the ways to watch live. Broadcast options depend on your country.

Why are there no MLS matches today?

MLS does not play every day. There are no games on many weekdays, and the league also pauses for international breaks and for the off-season between the MLS Cup and the start of the next season.

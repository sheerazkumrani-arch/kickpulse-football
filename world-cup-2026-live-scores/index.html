FIFA World Cup 2026: the short version

The FIFA World Cup 2026 was played in the United States, Canada and Mexico between 11 June and 19 July 2026. It was the biggest World Cup ever held, with 48 national teams and 104 matches, and the first one shared between three host countries. Spain won the tournament, beating defending champions Argentina 1-0 after extra time in the final at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey. Ferran Torres, who came on as a substitute, scored the winning goal in the 106th minute. England took third place with a 6-4 win over France in a wild bronze-medal match.

Facts worth knowing about World Cup 2026

  • The tournament ran for just over five weeks, from 11 June to 19 July 2026.
  • Forty-eight teams played, up from 32 in the previous World Cup.
  • The final was played at MetLife Stadium in the New York and New Jersey area.
  • Spain won their second World Cup, sixteen years after the first one in 2010. Argentina, the champions from 2022, finished second, and England finished third.
  • Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo, who have shaped the last two decades of football, were both part of the story, and for each of them it was almost certainly the last World Cup.

FIFA World Cup, football or soccer?

You will see the tournament described as the football World Cup in most countries and as the soccer World Cup in the United States and Canada. It is the same competition. Searches such as world cup football live score, FIFA live score or world cup soccer all lead to the same matches on KickPulse. If you prefer the American wording, our soccer live scores page covers it.

What comes after 2026

The next men's World Cup, in 2030, will be hosted by Spain, Portugal and Morocco, with a few early matches in South America to mark the 100th anniversary of the first tournament. The one after that, in 2034, is planned for Saudi Arabia. Between now and then there are continental qualifiers, friendlies and the Nations League, and all of them can be found on KickPulse, along with club football from the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and the other big leagues.

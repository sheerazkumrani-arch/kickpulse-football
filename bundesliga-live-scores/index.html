Bundesliga live scores and what makes the league different

The Bundesliga is Germany's top division and one of the most entertaining leagues to follow because the goals keep coming. It has 18 clubs rather than 20, so the season is 34 rounds long, and it takes a winter break in the middle. Bayern Munich have dominated the table for years, Borussia Dortmund are their most famous challengers, and the league has a long list of lively clubs with big, passionate stadiums.

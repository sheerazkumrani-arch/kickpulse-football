Italian football on one page

Serie A is the top flight of Italian football and has a reputation for tactical, tight matches where one goal can decide everything. Juventus, Inter, AC Milan, Napoli and Roma are the clubs most people know, and the league has plenty of smaller sides who are happy to spoil a favourite's afternoon. The derby between Inter and AC Milan, called the Derby della Madonnina, shares a stadium and splits a city, which makes it one of the loudest nights of the Italian calendar.

The season runs from late August to May with 20 clubs, and matches are spread through the weekend, with a few games on Friday and Monday.

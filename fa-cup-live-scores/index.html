The FA Cup: the oldest cup in football

The FA Cup is England's national knockout competition and the oldest in the world, first played in the 1871-72 season. Its charm is that clubs of every size can be drawn together, from the giants of the Premier League to teams from the lower divisions. A single match decides each tie, so a small club can knock out a famous one in a night, and these giant-killing results are why people search for FA Cup live scores with extra attention.

The competition runs from the autumn to May, with rounds played at weekends, and the final is held at Wembley Stadium in London.

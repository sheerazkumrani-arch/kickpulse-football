Following the Nations League

The UEFA Nations League is a competition for national teams. It began in the 2018-19 season, and Portugal won the first title. Teams are placed in leagues according to their level, so countries play opponents close to their own strength, and results can lead to promotion, relegation and a path into later qualifying. It takes place in the international breaks, which is why this page can feel quiet for weeks and then suddenly busy.

If you follow a national team, this is where you can see its next fixture, the live score once the game starts and the result afterwards. National team football also brings out the big names fans love to track, from Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo in their time to the young stars of Spain, France and England. Search for a country by name and the match for the selected date will come up.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Nations League scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Nations League match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

Why are there no Nations League matches today?

National team matches are played only in international windows. If today is empty, this page jumps to the next day with matches, or you can pick a date yourself.

Is KickPulse free to use for Nations League scores?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed and may be delayed or unavailable at times.

When is the Nations League played?

Nations League matches are played during international breaks, usually in autumn, spring and sometimes in June, with finals tournaments in the summer.

How is the Nations League different from the World Cup?

The Nations League is a European competition for UEFA members, while the FIFA World Cup is open to teams from every continent. Both are for national teams.

About UEFA Nations League live scores

The UEFA Nations League is a competition for European national teams played during the international match windows, when club football pauses. Because matches are grouped into a few days, it is useful to check the fixtures for the next dates. Use the date bar to move between match days.

National team football follows a different rhythm from club football, with several matches played in a short window and then a long gap. When there are no matches today, this page moves forward to the next day that has games, so you do not have to search for the schedule yourself. National teams that many fans follow include Spain, France, Germany, Portugal, Italy and England.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Nations League scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Nations League match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

Why are there no Nations League matches today?

National team matches are played only in international windows. If today is empty, this page jumps to the next day with matches, or you can pick a date yourself.

Is KickPulse free to use for Nations League scores?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed and may be delayed or unavailable at times.

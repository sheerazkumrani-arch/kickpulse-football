About UEFA Champions League live scores

The UEFA Champions League is Europe's top club competition, bringing together leading clubs from across the continent. Matches are usually played midweek in the European evening, which often means late-night or early-morning kick-offs in Pakistan. This page lists the matches for the selected date and shows every kick-off time in your own time zone.

The competition starts with a league phase, continues with knockout rounds that are mostly played over two legs, and ends with a single final. Because the match days change through the season, it helps to check the date bar for upcoming fixtures. When the data provider supplies a note such as the round or the leg, it is shown under the team names.

How the Champions League works, in plain words

The UEFA Champions League is the biggest club competition in Europe. Since the 2024-25 season it has used a league phase of 36 teams, where each club plays eight matches against different opponents instead of the old four-team groups. The best teams go straight to the round of 16, others fight through a play-off round, and from there it is knockout football all the way to the final in late May or early June.

Matches are usually played on Tuesday and Wednesday nights, with games kicking off at different times so you can follow more than one. The Live tab on this page lists every game being played right now, and the scores refresh about every 30 seconds. After the full-time whistle the result stays in the Finished tab, so you can come back later and still see how the night ended.

Real Madrid have won the competition more times than any other club, and the stories of comebacks, late goals and big upsets are a large part of why people check live scores here so often. Tap any match to see goals, cards, substitutions, shots, possession and lineups.

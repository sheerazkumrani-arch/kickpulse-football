About UEFA Champions League live scores

The UEFA Champions League is Europe's top club competition, bringing together leading clubs from across the continent. Matches are usually played midweek in the European evening, which often means late-night or early-morning kick-offs in Pakistan. This page lists the matches for the selected date and shows every kick-off time in your own time zone.

The competition starts with a league phase, continues with knockout rounds that are mostly played over two legs, and ends with a single final. Because the match days change through the season, it helps to check the date bar for upcoming fixtures. When the data provider supplies a note such as the round or the leg, it is shown under the team names.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

Kick-off times on this page are converted to your device's time zone automatically, so you always see your local time.

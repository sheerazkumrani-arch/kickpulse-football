Today's football, without the clutter

This page exists for one purpose: to answer what football is on today. It collects the matches from the Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, Nations League, the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, the Saudi Pro League, MLS, cups and plenty of other competitions, and puts them in a single list. Each match shows its status, the live minute, the score and the kick-off time. You can use the page the way you would use a printed fixture list, except that it updates itself.

Live football score today

When a match is being played, the score refreshes about every 30 seconds, so a goal shows up quickly. The Live tab removes everything else and shows only the games that are in play. If you want today's match score for one team, search for it. If you want the final scores only, open Finished. If you are planning ahead, open Upcoming and see which kick-offs are still to come tonight.

Your time zone, handled for you

Football is played all over the world, and kick-off times are usually announced in the local time of the host country. That makes it easy to get confused when you live somewhere else. KickPulse reads the time zone of your device and changes every time to match, then prints that time zone at the bottom of the page so you can check it. If you are in Pakistan, India, the Gulf, Europe or North America, the time you see is the time on your own clock.

A typical football day

  1. In the morning and early afternoon, you will often find games from Asia and Africa and the first games from Europe.
  2. Later in the afternoon and evening the big European leagues take over, with Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Bundesliga matches filling the list.
  3. On Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday evenings, European club competitions add the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League.
  4. Late at night in Europe it is the turn of the Americas, with MLS and the national team matches.

What you get for each match

Tap a match and a panel opens. It shows the events in order: goals, yellow and red cards and substitutions. You can compare the teams on possession, shots and corners, and check the lineups when they are published. The stadium and some basic match details appear when the data is there. You can share the match with a friend using the share buttons.

Is this a streaming site?

No, and we want to be clear about that. KickPulse shows scores and match information only. If you are looking for live football on TV or online, look for the official broadcaster or streaming service in your country. Our pages are meant to sit next to the broadcast, so you can check other games while one match is on screen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see all football matches today?

Open this page. The list shows today's matches from all leagues in your time zone. Use the Live tab for games in progress and the Upcoming tab for the ones still to start.

Why do the kick-off times look different from other websites?

KickPulse converts each time to your device's time zone. Some sites show the local time of the stadium, which can be several hours different from your own.

How do I find the score of one team?

Type the team name in the search box at the top. If the team has a match on the selected date it appears at the top of the list.

What if there is no football today?

Some days have few matches, especially between seasons or outside international breaks. Use the date bar to jump to the next day with matches.

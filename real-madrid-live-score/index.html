The club in a few facts

  • Real Madrid play at the Santiago Bernabeu Stadium in Madrid and are known for their white kit.
  • The club has won the European Cup and Champions League more times than any other, and is one of the most successful teams in La Liga.
  • The fixtures against Barcelona, called El Clasico, and against city rivals Atletico Madrid are among the most watched in world football.
  • Real Madrid has been the home of many of the biggest names in the game, including Cristiano Ronaldo, who left as the club's all-time top scorer.

How do I see the Real Madrid live score?

Which competitions does Real Madrid play in?

Real Madrid play in La Liga and the Spanish cup, and usually in the Champions League. They also take part in other tournaments, depending on the season.

Where does Real Madrid play home games?

Real Madrid play their home games at the Santiago Bernabeu Stadium in Madrid.

When do Real Madrid and Barcelona play each other?

They meet twice a season in La Liga, a match known as El Clasico. The dates are set in the league calendar, and they can also meet in cup competitions.

What does soccer mean, and why are there two names?

Soccer is a short form of association football, a name that was used in Britain in the nineteenth century to separate the game from rugby football. The short form stuck in the United States, Canada and some other countries, where football means a different sport. Elsewhere people just say football. The game, the rules and the clubs are the same, which is why you will see soccer reddit threads, soccer news, soccer highlights and live football score pages talking about the same matches. Major League Soccer and North American fans

MLS is the main professional league in the United States and Canada. It plays from February to the autumn, with the playoffs and the MLS Cup at the end. Lionel Messi's move to Inter Miami in 2023 drew a lot of new viewers to the league, and clubs such as LA Galaxy, LAFC, Seattle Sounders and Atlanta United have loyal local crowds.

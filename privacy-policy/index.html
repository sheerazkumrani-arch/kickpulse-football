Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

This policy explains what information KickPulse collects when you visit the website and how it is used. By using the website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

KickPulse does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name or email address. When you visit, our hosting and analytics services may record technical information such as the pages you view, your approximate country, the type of device and browser, and the website that sent you to us.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. These services describe their products as privacy-friendly and cookie-free. We use the results only to understand how many people visit and which pages are popular.

Advertising and the Meta (Facebook) pixel

We may use the Meta (Facebook) pixel to measure the results of our advertising and to show our ads to people who may be interested in football. The pixel can set cookies and sends information about your visit, such as the page you viewed, to Meta. Where the law requires it, the pixel is loaded only after you accept it in the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie choices link in the footer, or by clearing your browser data. You can also manage ad settings in your Facebook account.

Cookies and local storage

The website itself does not need cookies to work. If the cookie notice is shown, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that we do not ask you again on every visit.

Third-party services

To show scores your browser connects directly to ESPN's data servers, so ESPN receives your IP address and technical request details. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. When you use the share buttons, you leave KickPulse and the social network you choose applies its own privacy policy. Our hosting providers process basic request logs to deliver the website.

Children

KickPulse is a general football website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can decline the pixel, block cookies in your browser, or use a content blocker. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you. Because we do not collect names or email addresses, we cannot link information to you personally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, please contact us through the social links in the footer of this page.

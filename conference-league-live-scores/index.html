About UEFA Conference League live scores

The UEFA Conference League is UEFA's third club competition and gives clubs from many European countries a place in continental football. Matches are usually played on Thursdays. This page shows the live score, the match status and the kick-off time in your own time zone for every game on the selected date.

Because the competition is spread across many European countries, one match day can include a long list of games that kick off at different times. The Live tab above the list helps you jump straight to the matches that are being played right now, and the Finished tab shows completed results.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a date in the bar at the top. Today is selected by default, and this page moves ahead to the next match day when the competition has no games today.
  2. Use the All, Live, Finished and Upcoming tabs to narrow the list.
  3. Tap a match to open its timeline, statistics, lineups and match information.
  4. Search for a club or a competition in the search box.
  5. Share a match with friends using the share buttons.

Frequently asked questions

How often do Conference League scores update?

While matches are live, this page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. You can also press the refresh button at the top.

What can I see for each Conference League match?

Tap a match to open its details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics such as possession, shots and corners, and the lineups. Some details depend on what the data provider offers for that match.

Where can I see Conference League results?

Choose Yesterday or any earlier date in the date bar to see finished matches with their final scores.

Is KickPulse free to use for Conference League scores?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed and may be delayed or unavailable at times.

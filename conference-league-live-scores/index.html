The Conference League in a few lines

The UEFA Conference League is the newest of the three European club competitions. It started in the 2021-22 season and was created to give clubs from smaller football countries a real chance of playing European knockout football. Roma won the first edition. Since 2024-25 it has used a league phase like the other two competitions, and the matches are normally played on Thursday evenings.

For a fan, it is a good place to find football matches today that are not always in the spotlight, and a good page to keep open on a Thursday when several games are played at once. Conference League: quick facts and popular searches

The Conference League has a league phase of 36 clubs, and every club plays six matches. The clubs at the top move on to the knockout rounds and the next group plays a play-off.

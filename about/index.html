About KickPulse

KickPulse is a free football scores website built to make live scores quick and easy to follow on a phone or a computer. It brings matches from many leagues and cups into one clear page, so you do not have to open a different website for every competition.

What KickPulse offers

  • Live scores with the current match minute, updated while games are played.
  • Results from earlier days and fixtures for the days ahead.
  • Match details: a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics, lineups and stadium information when available.
  • Pages for popular competitions such as the Champions League, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Bundesliga.
  • Kick-off times in your own time zone, which is useful if you follow European or American matches from Pakistan or elsewhere in Asia.

Where the data comes from

Scores and match details come from ESPN's public data feed. The information is shown as it is received, and it can sometimes be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Some details, such as statistics and lineups, are only shown when the feed provides them for a match. KickPulse is an independent website and is not affiliated with ESPN, UEFA, FIFA or any league, club or national team.

Our approach

We keep the page light and fast, we do not show fake or sample matches, and when the data feed has a problem we keep showing the latest real scores and try again automatically. We count visits with privacy-friendly analytics so we can see which competitions people use most and improve the pages that matter.

Feedback

If you see a wrong score or want a competition added, please tell us through the social links in the footer of this page. Your feedback helps us improve KickPulse for everyone who loves football.

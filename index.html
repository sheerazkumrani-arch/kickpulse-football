Live football scores for every league

KickPulse is a fast, free way to follow football around the world. The page lists matches from the Champions League, Europa League, Nations League, the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and many more competitions, grouped by league and shown in your own time zone. Live matches show the current minute, finished matches show the final score, and upcoming matches show the kick-off time.

What you can do on KickPulse

  • See every live match now with one tap on the Live tab.
  • Browse results from yesterday and fixtures for the next days using the date bar or the calendar.
  • Open any match for its timeline of goals, cards and substitutions.
  • Compare team statistics such as possession, shots and corners when the data is available.
  • Check lineups, the stadium and other match information, then share the match with friends.

Frequently asked questions

Is KickPulse free to use?

Yes. KickPulse is free to use. Scores come from a public data feed, so they can be delayed or unavailable at times.

How often are the scores updated?

While matches are live, the page checks for new scores about every 30 seconds. At other times it checks about every 90 seconds, and you can press the refresh button at any moment.

Which leagues are covered?

The page covers the Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, Nations League, World Cup and qualifiers, the top European leagues, domestic cups, the Americas, Asia and Africa, plus many other competitions in the Other competitions group.

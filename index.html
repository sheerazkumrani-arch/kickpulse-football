Football today: how to find the match you want

Most people land here with the same simple question: what football is on right now? The Live tab answers that in one tap. It shows every match that is being played at this moment, with the minute on the clock and the current score. If nothing is live, switch to Upcoming and you will see the next kick-offs for the day, or use the date bar to jump a day ahead and check tomorrow's fixtures. The list is built for people who check scores many times a day, so it stays short, loads fast on a phone and does not bury the score under banners.

Every kick-off time on the page is converted to the time zone of your device. That sounds like a small thing until you follow a Premier League game or a Champions League night from Karachi, Dubai, London or New York and have to do the arithmetic in your head. Here you just read the time and plan your evening. The time zone we detected is printed at the bottom of every page, so you can see it is correct.

Football and soccer: same game, two names

In most of the world the sport is called football. In the United States and Canada it is called soccer, mostly to keep it apart from American football. Because many readers search in one word or the other, this site works for both. If you came here looking for live soccer, soccer scores or the latest soccer news from the big leagues, you are in the right place. What KickPulse is, and what it is not
